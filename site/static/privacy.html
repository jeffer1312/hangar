Hangar Privacy Policy

Updated November 4, 2026 · Português

Hangar is an app for controlling, from your phone, coding agents that run on your own servers. The developer operates no server for the app, has no user accounts and does not receive your conversations or files.

What stays on the device

Addresses and credentials of your servers, kept in the system's secure storage, and a device identifier created by the app.

What goes to your server

Messages, attachments you choose, dictation or voice audio, that identifier and the notification token. You control the server, and what it does with this data, including sending it to AI or transcription providers you configure, follows your configuration.

Permissions

Microphone for dictation and voice; camera to read connection QR codes; photos and files only when you attach something; notifications for alerts from your servers.

Third parties

Notifications pass through Expo and Google (Firebase Cloud Messaging). App updates come from Expo, which receives technical data such as IP address and app version. We use no ads or analytics tools, and we do not sell data.

Deletion

Uninstalling the app deletes its data from the device. Data on your server stays under your control.

Children

The app is not intended for children under 13.
